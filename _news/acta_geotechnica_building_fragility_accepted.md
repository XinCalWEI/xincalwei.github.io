I’m pleased to share that our paper on building fragility under slow-moving landslides has been **accepted for publication in _Acta Geotechnica_**.

**Spatiotemporal fragility assessment of buildings affected by slow-moving landslides using InSAR-derived deformation and Bayesian updating**

**Authors:** [Lin Tan](https://www.terramosaic.org/people/lin-tan/), [Lulu Zhang](https://www.terramosaic.org/people/lulu-zhang/), [Te Xiao](https://www.terramosaic.org/people/te-xiao/), Yinghui Tian, **Xin Wei**, Zhihua Zhang, Dongsheng Liu, Chunlan Du, and Hai Li.

**Corresponding authors:** [Lulu Zhang](https://www.terramosaic.org/people/lulu-zhang/) and [Te Xiao](https://www.terramosaic.org/people/te-xiao/).
