**November 13–14, 2025**
**U-M Annual Data Science & AI Summit 2025**

---

Last week, MIDAS brought together the data science and AI community for an inspiring two-day U-M Annual Data Science & AI Summit, the largest annual gathering of researchers, students, and collaborators advancing the future of AI and data science.

Throughout the summit, attendees explored:

- Cutting-edge Research Vision Talks covering responsible AI, scientific discovery, health, engineering, governance, and more
- Conversations about how data and AI are reshaping every field—from astrophysics to public policy to medicine
- Opportunities to connect with collaborators across disciplines
- A forward-looking view of U-M's leadership in the era of AI
- Poster sessions highlighting innovative student and faculty work
