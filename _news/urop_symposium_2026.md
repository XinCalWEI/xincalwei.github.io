**April 22, 2026 · Michigan League, Ann Arbor, Michigan**

The Undergraduate Research Opportunity Program's Annual Research Symposium marks the culmination of a full academic year of research. Students share their projects, learn from one another, and celebrate the partnerships they have built with their mentors. The 2026 Spring Symposium was held on Wednesday, April 22, at the Michigan League.

All three undergraduate students I mentored through UROP during the 2025–2026 academic year presented posters on our generative-AI landslide research:
