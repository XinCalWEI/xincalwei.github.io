The Body Keeps the Score: Brain, Mind, and Body in the Healing of Trauma

Bessel van der Kolk  ·  2014

 ·  non-fiction  ·  psychology   trauma   mental-health   neuroscience


Dr. Bessel van der Kolk draws on decades of experience to argue persuasively that trauma literally reshapes both body and brain. He explores innovative treatments that offer new paths to recovery by engaging the brain’s neuroplasticity.
